# Orxu

# PROJECT BRIEF — ORXU CORPORATE WEBSITE

Act as a senior brand strategist, digital product designer, UX/UI designer, copywriter and frontend engineer.

Your task is to research, plan, design and fully develop the official corporate website for **ORXU**, an independent technology company.

This is a complete website project, not a simple landing page, template customization or generic SaaS website.

You are responsible for the entire process, from design research and visual direction to implementation, responsive behavior, content, interactions, SEO and final quality assurance.

## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

Company name: ORXU

Business category: Independent technology company.

ORXU operates through two complementary business areas:

**A. Proprietary digital products**

ORXU conceives, develops, owns, manages and commercializes its own software products, digital platforms and technological solutions.

**B. Custom technology development**

ORXU also develops websites, web applications, software systems and digital platforms for individuals, businesses and organizations. It can additionally provide ongoing technical management, maintenance and improvements.

The website must communicate both business areas equally clearly.

Do not position ORXU exclusively as:
- A SaaS company.
- A web design agency.
- A digital marketing agency.
- An AI automation agency.
- A freelance development service.

ORXU is a technology company that builds its own products and develops technological solutions for external clients.

## 2. DESIGN RESEARCH — MANDATORY BEFORE IMPLEMENTATION

Do not immediately start coding.

First, investigate contemporary, high-quality corporate technology websites and real design references.

Search for references from:
- Modern independent technology companies.
- Established software development companies.
- Premium software product studios.
- Innovative digital product companies.
- Professional technology brands with strong visual identities.

Study websites such as Linear, Vercel, Stripe, Raycast, Framer and other relevant contemporary companies. These are research starting points, not templates to copy.

Investigate:
- Current typography and typographic hierarchy.
- Modern navigation patterns.
- Distinctive hero compositions.
- Sophisticated grid systems.
- Layout proportions and whitespace.
- Product presentation.
- Corporate storytelling.
- Subtle motion and interaction.
- Footer structures.
- Responsive behavior.
- Use of color, contrast and visual depth.

Look for current design references, not outdated templates or generic website examples.

Avoid designs that look like conventional templates from several years ago.

Do not copy any reference website. Extract useful design principles and synthesize a distinctive visual identity for ORXU.

Before implementation, establish a clear visual direction and design system.

If reference-research tools are unavailable, use accessible real websites and document the references you actually inspected. Never pretend to have researched a reference you could not access.
